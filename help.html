Harassment support guide

  • Do not respond if it could increase risk; seek support from someone you trust.
  • Preserve screenshots showing account name, full message, date and time.
  • Save profile URLs, usernames, phone numbers, payment requests and message links.
  • Keep originals where possible; do not edit screenshots. Store copies somewhere safe.
  • Use the platform’s block and report tools. Report serious threats to the appropriate authorities.
  • If you face immediate physical danger, open SOS or call emergency assistance.

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Verify official contacts and availability for your region.
